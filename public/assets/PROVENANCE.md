# Original artwork

All five assets were produced with the built-in ImageGen tool for this project on 2026-09-27, then encoded as JPEG for delivery. The generated originals remain in the local Codex generated_images folder. No stock artwork is used.

## landscape.jpg

Prompt: Use case: stylized-concept. Create a masterful classical Chinese ink wash landscape painting for a Tang poetry interactive website. Very wide panoramic landscape. Refined Song dynasty literati shanshui painting on nearly white cool rice paper, subtle organic fibers. Layered distant misty mountains in pale ink gray and muted celadon, a dark detailed pine and rocky shore on the far left bottom edge, small river boat toward bottom center, fine low river banks across lower edge. Composition: artwork concentrated in left third and lower quarter, right two thirds and top half spacious luminous unpainted paper, for readable data charts overlay. Elegant actual ink brushwork, dry brush rocks and wet washes, restrained mineral sage green. No text, calligraphy, red seals, letters, UI, borders or watermarks. Wide 3:2 or 16:9 image. This is original production artwork, not a website mockup.

## blossom.jpg

Prompt: Original refined classical Chinese literati ink painting of a single graceful flowering plum branch, dark dry-brush curving branches extend from lower left corner upward along left side, small muted vermilion and dusty rose blossoms, a few pale sage leaves. Landscape format. Almost-white cool rice paper background. Delicate masterful brushwork and botanical shapes, large unpainted empty right two thirds and upper half. Bottom left is richly painted, rest spacious. No text, stamps, symbols, frame or UI. Production background for Tang poetry website, sophisticated Chinese ink wash, organic detailed pigment.

## landscape-waterfall.jpg

Prompt: Traditional Chinese ink-and-light-color panoramic website background matching the existing paper and brush character. A tall waterfall descends from a dark cliff at the far right into a broad river across the lower third; mist separates layered peaks, with a tiny stone bridge and distant pavilion. Refined Song-inspired shanshui on fibrous off-white xuan paper, pale mineral blue-green and restrained black ink. Central and left areas remain calm for interface content. No text, calligraphy, seals, frame, modern objects, or watermark.

## sky-moon.jpg

Prompt: Traditional Chinese ink panoramic sky painting matching the existing assets. An immense evening sky fills the composition; a luminous full moon sits in the upper right behind layered drifting clouds, with a low ridge, reed shore, distant rain and migrating geese. Diluted indigo-gray and silver-beige washes on fibrous off-white xuan paper. No text, calligraphy, seals, frame, modern objects, or watermark.

## color-pigments.jpg

Prompt: Abstract but authentic Chinese ink-and-mineral-pigment panoramic painting matching the existing paper and brush quality. Low misty hills and a quiet river sit beneath translucent vermilion, malachite, azurite, ochre, violet and ink-black capillary blooms. Pigments concentrate around the outer thirds and lower edge, leaving the center pale for interface content. No text, calligraphy, seals, frame, modern objects, or watermark.
